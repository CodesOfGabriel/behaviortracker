# Visual and audiovisual asset inventory

Reviewed for 0.1.7 on 2026-11-27. The maintainer states that **they created all
images and icons** and reports AI-assisted visual development. This authorship
declaration is recorded without inventing per-file generation tools, prompts,
external sources or licenses. All original images, icons, GIF, video, diagrams,
institutional logo renderings and application styling are preserved.

## Provenance categories

- **Maintainer-created project artwork:** authorship reported by the maintainer.
- **AI-assisted artwork:** assistance reported globally; no per-file generation
  history was supplied, so individual files are not falsely classified as wholly
  human-created or wholly AI-generated.
- **Third-party images/videos:** no particular external original has been
  identified by the maintainer for these files; no external license is invented.
- **Institutional marks:** UFPel, NeuroCell and NeuroLab renderings are identified
  separately from artwork authorship. Institutional identity/trademark rights
  are not transferred by the Apache grant for application code.

The project code license and permission to render institutional marks have
different scopes. No additional asset license has been assigned automatically
by this review. Any future asset-specific terms should be approved by the
maintainer and recorded here, without replacing the existing artwork.

## Other visual material

Development screenshots and study-derived images under `artifacts/` and
`docs/reports/` are retained. Study data and derived images have a different
rights/provenance review from authored application artwork; see
[DATASET_CARD.md](DATASET_CARD.md). Any approved omission of study-derived files
from the source archive is separate from these preserved application assets;
see [the exact archive plan](docs/releases/v0.1.7-archive-plan.json).

The inline Google sign-in mark and operating-system symbols are functional
vendor identifiers in application code; this inventory does not claim their
trademarks or waive the respective brand terms. Lucide icons remain subject to
their upstream ISC terms, recorded in the dependency inventory.

A file hash records the preserved bytes, not a finding of ownership or an
independent verification of the maintainer's creation process.
